{%- comment -%} Renders a post's or page's body. Authors write root-relative paths such as /assets/img/posts//figure-1.png; when the site is served under a base path (for example jmlrorg.github.io/tmlr-blog/ before the custom domain is in place), those paths are prefixed with it. Protocol-relative URLs (//cdn...) are left alone. With an empty baseurl nothing changes. Usage: {% include content.html body=content %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign body = include.body -%} {%- if site.baseurl and site.baseurl != "" -%} {%- assign src_prefix = 'src="' | append: site.baseurl | append: '/' -%} {%- assign href_prefix = 'href="' | append: site.baseurl | append: '/' -%} {%- assign body = body | replace: 'src="//', 'src="PROTOCOLRELATIVE' | replace: 'href="//', 'href="PROTOCOLRELATIVE' | replace: 'src="/', src_prefix | replace: 'href="/', href_prefix | replace: 'src="PROTOCOLRELATIVE', 'src="//' | replace: 'href="PROTOCOLRELATIVE', 'href="//' -%} {%- endif -%} {{ body }}
