We are excited to launch [**TMLR Beyond PDF**](http://tmlr-beyond-pdf.org)!

TMLR Beyond PDF is a new, HTML-based submission format for TMLR, that supports interactive figures and videos, along with the usual LaTeX and images. This format is inspired by interactive publication formats such as [Distill.pub](https://distill.pub/) and [ICLR Blogposts](https://iclr-blogposts.github.io/2025/about/).

![](/assets/img/posts/tmlr-beyond-pdf/figure-2.png)

The submission process for TMLR Beyond PDF is largely the same as standard TMLR. Submissions are made on [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/group?id=TMLR) to ensure that the peer-review process remains double-blind. To make a Beyond PDF submission, just select the brand new **“Beyond PDF submission (pageless, webpage-style content)”** submission type and upload a zipped folder containing your submission files ([the author kit can be found here](https://tmlr-beyond-pdf.org/assets/tmlr-beyond-pdf-author-kit.zip)).

![](/assets/img/posts/tmlr-beyond-pdf/figure-3.png)

Once you submit to TMLR Beyond PDFs, your submission will be rendered automatically at [https://tmlr-beyond-pdf.org/under\_review](https://tmlr-beyond-pdf.org/under_review/)!

Check out <https://tmlr-beyond-pdf.org/submission-instructions> for step by step instructions. Also check out the [TMLR website](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/) for more details on TMLR’s editorial policies, review process, and other information that applies to both conventional and Beyond PDF submissions.

Special thanks to the OpenReview team, in particular Celeste Martinez and Melisa Bok, for their help!

We also extend our thanks to the committee led by Pablo Samuel Castro that made recommendations regarding the extension of TMLR to the Beyond PDF format. The committee included Susan Zhang, Fabian Pedregosa, Nikhil Thorat, Thomas Dietterich, Sara Hooker, Hugo Larochelle, and Paul Vicol.

We look forward to seeing you at [TMLR Beyond PDF](https://tmlr-beyond-pdf.org/)!
